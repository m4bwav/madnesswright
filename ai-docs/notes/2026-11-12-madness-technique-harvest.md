## Techniques (ranked by usefulness)

20. **[Built 2026-11-12, plugin 0.1.24; in-game test owed]** Animation Events catalogue for custom animation clips.** Clips take event nodes of two kinds, "Run Events" and "Method Events" (functionally the same; only Method Events are listed). Melee: `H2H_Warmup` (pull-back, INT 0 right / 1 left hand), `H2H_Swing_M` (swing starts; the counter check happens here), `H2H_Swing_U` (unarmed; INT 0-4 = right hand, left hand, right foot, left foot, headbutt), `_Heavy` variants, `H2H_SwingEnd`, `H2H_Hit_M`/`_U` (the strike frame), `H2H_Stab_M`/`_U` (Piercing damage), `H2H_Bite`, `H2H_Kick` (special knockback), `H2H_Sweep`/`H2H_SweepUnarmed`/`H2H_SweepEnd` (hit everyone in the arc; heavy attacks sweep automatically), `H2H_DashAttack` (INT 1 weak, 2 strong), `H2H_Advance` (FLOAT speed push toward target or cursor), and `H2H_End`, which marks where the next combo swing starts (ignored if the player does not continue). Ranged/sync: `ShootEvent` (+`_Head`, `_Body`; hits the sync partner, fails outside a sync anim), `ShootEvent_Sourced_ByName` (fire as a named MadCard_Ranged/Thrown), `WeaponEvent_Degrade` (INT damage to weapon condition), `H2H_GrappleSynch_ByName` (start a grapple using a named Animations MadCard, v2.13.i), `ExecuteEvent` (STRING execution category). Spawning: `SpawningComplete` (release the entrance early), `EnterComplete`, `TriggerEntranceAddonEffects`, `EndAnimUpdateRoot` (stop the Hips sliding back when a clip ends off its start), `SpawnEndUpdateRoot`. Sound/voice: `SoundEvent` (FMOD event name), `SoundEvent_Hands`, `VoiceEvent` (INT 0-9: Angry, Attack, BigAttack, Cheer, Laugh, Grunt, Death, Pain, Affirmative, Dialogue), `VoiceEvent_20` (20 % chance), `JumpEvent`, `WeaponSoundEvent` (INT 0-5: Equip, Stow, Drop, Block, Hit, Swing), `Footstep`, `LandEvent`. Misc: `AnimEvent` (play another clip), `GibMe`. Why: a custom attack without `H2H_Warmup`/`Swing`/`Hit`/`End` at the right frames will not deal damage or chain. Skill: madnesswright-modtools (new reference, e.g. references/animation-events.md). Source: Custom Animations doc, part 4. Confidence: stated (draft doc; `ShootEvent_Body` is described as "targets their head", a copy slip). In-game test: yes, one custom swing with the four core events.

21. **[Built 2026-11-12, plugin 0.1.24; in-game test owed]** Scripted effects from animation frames.** `CreationEvent_ByName` spawns a ParticleSystem or ModTools GameObject at the feet, with `_Center`, `_Head`, `_Chest`, `_HandR`, `_HandL` and `_Center_ParentCapsule` (parented to the character's collider) variants (v2.13.i). `SplodeEvent_ByName` / `SplodeEvent_Friendly_ByName` / `SplodeEvent_Friendly_Hand_ByName` create a named MadCard_Explosion (friendly ones spare allies); `SlamEvent`, `SlamEvent_Sourced`, `SplodeDeath` (remove the character, explode in its place), `GibSplode`, `GibEvent`. Effects: `CamShakeEvent` (FLOAT 0-25), `SlowMoEvent_PlayerSquad`, `ChatterEvent` (INT 0-9 chatter categories), `SpeakEvent` (STRING custom line). Why: boss entrances, suicide units and ground slams without Harmony code. Skill: madnesswright-modtools, cross-link from madnesswright (MadCard_Explosion). Source: Custom Animations doc, part 4. Confidence: stated. In-game test: yes.

22. **[Built 2026-11-12, plugin 0.1.24; in-game test owed]** Custom animation pipeline outline.** Rig is the M:PN Blender Sample Project armature made in Blender 2.79; do not change the armature ("problems will ensue"). Export .fbx, never .blend. Mind the Sync and Root nodes. Scale 0.45 in Unity. Events are added in the ModTools project (Unity 2019.2.6f). Clip types: Sync Anim, Attack (Warmup/Swing/Hit/End, Sweep), Entrance. Part 3 (the Animations MadCard format for new attacks, grapples, executions) is a placeholder only. Skill: madnesswright-modtools. Source: Custom Animations doc, parts 1-3 (outline bullets). Confidence: stated, but terse; the 0.45 figure is unexplained (import Scale Factor or object scale). In-game test: yes, before writing the 0.45 rule as fact.

23. **[Built 2026-11-12, plugin 0.1.24; in-game test owed]** PX_Door must be a child of Room_Layout or it does not work.** The video's author also puts Player_Start and the Squad_Spawner under Characters, and says cover must be inside the Room_Layout. The skill already places Player_Start under Characters (S02), but says nothing about door parenting, and the Squad_Spawner row says only "position does not matter". Skill: madnesswright-scenes (scenetools-rules.md table, PX_Door row; maybe a lint in mpn.py). Source: video _xbtGGh6Rso, ~9 min. Confidence: stated for doors; "I believe" for the spawner. In-game test: cheap, one door moved out of the layout.

24. **[Built 2026-11-12, plugin 0.1.24; in-game test owed]** The floor must not extend past the back wall.** If the floor plane runs behind the back-wall quad, characters can teleport behind the wall. Size the floor to stop at the wall. Skill: madnesswright-scenes (SKILL step 2, geometry). Source: video _xbtGGh6Rso, ~3 min. Confidence: stated by a community author from experience; mechanism inferred (spawn or pathing snaps to Floor-tagged surface out of sight). In-game test: optional.

25. **[Built 2026-11-12, plugin 0.1.24; in-game test owed]** The "bib": quads that hide the void.** Copies of the back-wall quad placed just under the front floor edge, above the walls and down the sides, with a Room_Cutaway material, block the skybox/void from the Dev_Cam view. Open problem from the video: the top bib casts a shadow on the room (turn off its shadow casting, inferred fix). The fourth wall is a 180-degree quad copy with its Mesh Renderer removed. Skill: madnesswright-scenes (name the term; LEARNINGS already covers cutaway materials and the box fourth wall). Source: video _xbtGGh6Rso, ~5-7 min. Confidence: observed in the video; shadow fix inferred. In-game test: shadow fix only.

26. **[Built 2026-11-12, plugin 0.1.24; in-game test owed]** Squad_Spawner fields seen in the editor.** Wave Spawn Type "Mission Waves" takes a Start text, a subtext and an End text shown on screen (example "You won"). Typing "Manual character" in a Units entry fills in a manual character. A boss entry takes a typed weapon name. A named Weapon Stockpile preset (e.g. Melee Tier 2, said to hold 17 weapons) caps that entry at its weapon count; to arm more units add another entry. Spawn doors are assigned by dragging each door into the spawner's door list. Skill: madnesswright-scenes (scenetools-rules.md, Squad_Spawner). Source: video _xbtGGh6Rso, ~12-15 min. Confidence: observed for the text fields; the stockpile cap is the author's "from what I understand". In-game test: yes for the cap.
