# Director (was Playground Director, and before that Swap And Unaware)

Direct any fight, in any mode: the story campaign, the arenas, custom stages and the Playground. Take control of any character, freeze the room, set patrols and guards, pose enemies, clear the bodies and save scenes to replay. Renamed from Playground Director in 0.6.1 because it was never Playground-only. A requested mod, kept here as the worked example for the [madnesswright-modtools](../../skills/madnesswright-modtools/SKILL.md) skill's Harmony route: an `IModEntrypoint`, two Harmony prefixes, one component per feature, a net48 build against the player's own game files, and the bugs live playtests found.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| INSERT | The Director menu: pauses the game and lists every action below as a button with its key, plus **Clear corpses** and **Clear blood, bullet holes, casings and debris**, which have no key. Actions aimed at one character ask you to click it after the menu closes (right click cancels). INSERT or ESC closes the menu. |
| F1 | Remove every living character except you, your F8 home body and the playable characters (player controlled, the career's core roster, or a Player-faction card such as Sanford, Deimos or Hank), the way the Playground's Clear All removes them. |
| F2 | Freeze everyone but you (AI, movement, animation). F2 again unfreezes the ones it froze. A frozen character still dies when shot. |
| F3 | Patrol squares (Ancient Warfare 3 style). A black square follows the mouse; left click drops it; U starts another; click a square to select it (yellow outline), then P makes it red (a stop) or black again and Delete removes it. F3 hides and shows them. They belong to the body you control when you place them and only show while you control it. Whenever that body is an NPC, unaware and not frozen or guarding, it walks them in a loop and halts at a red one. While a square is being placed or the mouse is on one, left click does not attack. |
| F4 | On the character under the mouse: a list of idle animations (its own idle emotes, then every looping clip in the game's library, with a filter box). Click one to play it; the character stops wandering while it poses. F4 or ESC closes. |
| F5 | Take control of the character nearest the mouse pointer on screen: a squadmate or an enemy, in any room (through locked arena doors too). Your old body becomes an NPC. Works while you are dead. Lets go of a freeze or guard on that body first. |
| F6 | Every other character goes back to unaware and forgets who it was fighting. They can notice you again. |
| F7 | Keep everyone unaware (re-applied twice a second) until you press F7 again. |
| F8 | Back to your own body (the one you had before the first F5). Happens by itself when the body you took dies, if your own body is still alive. |
| F9 | On the character under the mouse: stands guard. It never moves, goes unaware, and fights anyone not on its team once it sees them (the game's own FixedInPlace spawner option). F9 again: back to normal. |
| F10 | On the character under the mouse: unaware goes to search, search goes to attack (you are its target), attack goes to unaware. |
| F11 | While paused (story, arena, custom stage or Playground): name the scene, ENTER saves it. CAPS LOCK while paused lists saved scenes, this stage's first; click one to load it; ESC closes. See "Scenes" below for what loading does where. |

On the Steam Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3822329165 (Subscribe, then enable it from the gear icon).

Needs the `releasebeta` branch: the normal release build does not load mods. Version 0.3.0 was playtested in the Playground on two PCs; 0.4.0 on the owner's PC on 2026-10-21, in the Playground and then the story mode (F9 and F11 not yet exercised), and was on the Workshop until 0.6.0. 0.5.0 (scenes in every level) failed its first story playtest (scene files lost the characters); 0.5.1 fixes it and was playtested in a story level on the owner's PC on 2026-10-22 (arenas, Playground scenes and F9 not yet exercised); never on the Workshop. 0.6.0 adds the Director menu and clear corpses (built 2026-10-22, loads cleanly, published at the owner's request before its playtest); 0.6.1 only renames it to Director and is on the Workshop. 0.7.0 makes loading a scene reset weapons and items too; its first story play (2026-10-22) reset the floor weapons but not the ammo in the player's shotgun, and 0.7.1 fixes that; its play showed a thrown shotgun vanished on load, and 0.7.2 picks it back up. 0.7.3 and 0.7.4 (bodies and blood on load) passed a story play on 2026-10-22 and 0.7.4 is on the Workshop. 0.7.5 (teammates fighting after a load, lost animations) passed a Playground and story play on 2026-10-24. 0.7.6 skips the after-load checks when the stage changed before they ran; 0.8.0 adds two players (the game's local co-op); both built and deployed, not yet played. Back up your saves before using it in a story career.

## Key choices

- The Director menu opens with INSERT (0.6.0). A fresh decompile lists INSERT only in the `-devtools` mod-test tools, so it is free in play; F12 (Steam's screenshot key) and CAPS LOCK (the scene list) were ruled out. INSERT works paused or not: the menu holds its own pause. Clear corpses and clear debris got no key: no free key was obvious, and the menu is one press away.
- Clear corpses keeps bodies a level may still need: your squad and the playable characters (a story squadmate can be revived), bosses, cutscene actors, the level's scripted characters (serial numbers) and anything the game marks essential. Bodies dead under a second are left too. Clearing blood and debris was the same four calls as the Playground's Clear All, so it is a second button.

- The requester asked for F1 to F4 and F9 to F11 and to leave F5 to F8 and F12 alone ("Do not make a button that is not F5, F6, F7, F8, and F12" read as "do not take those"). F12 is Steam's screenshot key.
- No key moved. In a fresh decompile (`decomp.py keys`), F1 to F4, F9 to F11, P and Delete are read only by the developer console (debug builds), the `-devtools` mod-test tools, and the profile window's tab keys while it is open; U and Return are unread in play. CapsLock is the game's autowalk key; the mod reads it only while paused, but the game may still toggle autowalk (any move key turns it off).
- Scenes are files in `AppData/LocalLow/Gibbing Tree/Madness Project Nexus/PlaygroundDirector/Scenes` under the user's profile, outside `Mods/`, because the game reads every `.json` in a mod folder as a MadCard. 0.4.0 scene files still load.

## Clear corpses (0.6.0)

- Removes dead bodies in every room of the level the way the game's own developer console does (`HaveRunKillMe`, then `Decay_Manager.DestroyMe_Character`, which the arenas also use), and writes `cleared N corpses in room X and M in other rooms; kept K` to Player.log. The living, you, and kill counts are not touched: kills were counted when they died.
- A scene saved with a character who has since died still respawns it on load; the corpse is not needed.

## Scenes (0.5.0; weapons and items since 0.7.0, bodies since 0.7.3, blood since 0.7.4, teams and animations since 0.7.5)

Why, and what was left out: [the decision](../../ai-docs/decisions/2026-10-21-scenes-in-every-level.md).

- **Playground:** as in 0.4.0. Loading clears the room (the game's Clear All) and spawns the saved characters, each in its saved room if that room is still there; you keep your body and place. A scene saved on another Playground stage loads that stage first.
- **Story levels, arenas, custom stages, hubs:** loading moves you back to the spot and room where you saved, waits a moment for that room's own characters, then puts the characters already there back the way they were (position, facing, damage, alert, frozen, guard, patrol, and since 0.7.0 their weapons), respawns the ones who have died since, and removes characters the scene does not hold. It never removes your squad, the playable characters, the level's scripted characters (the ones its triggers refer to), bosses or cutscene actors; bosses and cutscene actors are not saved at all.
- **Weapons and items (0.7.0):** a scene keeps what each character holds in its hands and holsters and its grenade, with the ammo left in each, plus every weapon or item lying loose in a room. Loading gives each character back exactly that, clears loose weapons from the open rooms (including ones dropped by the dead since the save) and lays the saved ones back where they were. Your own weapons are swapped back only in the Playground: in a story or arena career your weapons belong to the career save, so there (and for any squadmate holding a career weapon) the weapons stay and only their ammo goes back to the saved count, for each weapon still in the same hand or holster (0.7.1). A weapon thrown or dropped since the save is picked back up into its hand or holster from wherever it lies, or replaced with a fresh one if it is gone (0.7.2). Quest items are never touched. Rooms the game has locked keep their weapons. Scenes saved before 0.7.0 load the characters as before and say to save again for weapons.
- **Bodies (0.7.3):** a scene remembers which bodies were already lying in the level. Loading clears the bodies of everyone who died after the save, including the old bodies of the characters it respawns, and keeps the older ones. Your squad's, the playable characters', the level's scripted characters', bosses' and essential bodies always stay (as with Clear corpses). Scenes saved before 0.7.3 clear only the old bodies of the respawned.
- **Blood (0.7.4):** loading in a story level or arena removes the blood, bullet holes, casings and debris made since the save, and keeps the older ones while the level stays loaded. After a restart of the stage or the game, it removes them all (the ones from before the save are gone by then anyway). The Playground load already clears them all. Blood is not painted back.
- **Teams (0.7.5):** a character respawned by a load joins the squad its saved squadmates are in now, instead of a new squad of its own. Before 0.7.5 a respawned member of a squad with survivors got a fresh squad, and the game counts anyone outside your squad as a foe when the faction is None (all Playground spawns), so teammates fought each other after a load. A character put back in a squad that would fight its saved squadmates (a None faction, or a faction changed since the save) joins theirs too; your squad and essential characters are never moved. In the Playground, saved characters still standing in another room (Clear All empties only yours) are put back rather than doubled. Each load line names the character's squad and faction now and at save, and 2 s later Player.log names any pair of saved squadmates hostile to each other.
- **Animations (0.7.5):** a scene remembers the looping animation each character was playing (F4's idle animations and the level's own, such as typing or smoking), and a load starts it again half a second later, for characters still unaware. Small ruling: one-off moves (a swing, a reload, a dive) are not replayed; the fight starts them again on its own. A frozen (F2) character is released for a moment, starts the animation and is frozen again. Scenes saved before 0.7.5 load without animations; save them again.
- **Standing still on load (0.7.3):** a character put back drops the walk orders it got after the save (from the fight or a trigger), so it does not walk straight off its spot; the level's standing orders, such as a squadmate following you, stay. Level props held by characters (the laborers' crate) are given back too.
- Each story or arena load writes one Player.log line per saved character (put back or respawned, how it was matched, how far it moved, which weapons it got and which failed) and, 2 s later, a line for each character more than 1.5 m off its saved spot. If the stage changed in those 2 s (you left the level), the check is skipped with one line instead (0.7.6).
- Story levels lock each room you leave. If the scene's room is closed, restart the stage (pause, Restart) and load again: from the start, the mod takes you straight to that room.
- A story, arena or custom scene saved on another stage is listed with that stage's name in brackets. Play that stage, then load it: starting a stage writes your career save, so the mod does not do it.
- Removing a character counts as a kill for the level (kill counters, arena kill count). Doors, triggers and objectives are not rewound.
- Arenas cannot be paused between waves (the game blocks it): save and load mid-wave or before the first wave.
- Every save and load writes one line to Player.log (stage, room, counts), so a playtest can be checked from the log.
- Folder, DLL and HarmonyID stay `SwapUnaware`, so the enabled state and the Workshop item carry over; only the display name changed (Playground Director in 0.4.0, Director in 0.6.1). The Workshop title keeps "(was Playground Director)" so subscribers recognise it, and the Playground tag was dropped.

## Two players (0.8.0)

The game has local co-op: press **Start** on a pad to join (answer No to "Control Player One?" to keep the keyboard player as player one); **Start with both shoulder buttons** leaves. A joining player takes a free member of your squad; with none free, they join at the next door, where story stages add a bonus character. In the Playground, put a squadmate in your squad first; in arenas see [Arena co-op](#arena-co-op-090). Game side: [the research note](../../ai-docs/notes/2026-10-24-two-player-research.md); rulings: [the plan](../../ai-docs/plans/2026-10-24-two-player.md).

- **The keyboard player drives the Director** (small ruling): every key and mouse pick acts for player one. A pad player gets no Director buttons. F10's "attacks you" means the keyboard player.
- No feature touches another player's character: F2 never freezes it, F9 lets go of a guard a player takes over, the click block for patrol squares and menu picks stops only the keyboard player's shots, Clear corpses keeps a player's body. F8 says so when another player has taken your home body.
- **Scenes** (file version 6) keep the other players' characters. A load puts a character a player plays now back on its spot with its saved weapons (only the ammo in a story or arena career) and nothing else: no alert, freeze or squad change, never a respawn or removal. A player who joined after the save stays where they are; a character nobody plays any more loads as a squadmate. The Playground load clears the room itself instead of the game's Clear All, which removes every character but player one's.
- Player.log: `player 2 joined on pad 0`, `player 2 left`, `player 2 now plays <name> (<squad>)`, and one `played by player 2: put back` line per player on a scene load.

## Arena co-op (0.9.0)

The game builds an arena career around one character with one origin, and a pad that presses Start in an arena waits for a free squadmate that a young career never has (hired squadmates come only after the pilot rescue). Director fills the gap with a **guest**: press Start on the pad (answer No to "Control Player One?"), and a panel that pad drives lists the origins your career has unlocked, with their traits and bonuses. D-pad browses, **A** picks, **B** cancels (leave and press Start again to reopen it). The guest appears beside you at your level with the origin's armour and starter weapon. Game side: [the research note](../../ai-docs/notes/2026-10-24-arena-coop-research.md); routes and rulings: [the plan](../../ai-docs/plans/2026-10-24-arena-coop.md).

- **The guest is not part of your career** (small ruling): cash, XP, tokens, checkpoints and stats stay yours as now, and the guest is gone in the HQ.
- **Player two's death never ends the wave**; the guest is back beside you once the wave is over. **Your death still ends the wave**, as in the game: the arena has no revive.
- Only in arena stages, never the HQ (story levels and the Playground have the game's own co-op). If you brought a hired squadmate, the game hands it to the pad first and no panel shows.
- Leaving (Start with both shoulders) takes the guest away with a poof. The last pick of each pad is remembered in `PlaygroundDirector/arena-guests.txt` and offered first next time.
- Every Director feature treats the guest as a player (0.8.0's rules), and scenes save it like any other player's character.
- Player.log: `arena guests: player 2 is picking an origin`, `arena guest: player 2 picked <origin>`, `arena guest: player 2 plays <name> (<origin>, level N, <weapon>)`, and a line when it is removed.

## Install

From a zip made by `pack.ps1`:

1. Unzip it anywhere on the computer that plays.
2. Double-click `install.cmd`. It copies the `SwapUnaware` folder into `%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Gibbing Tree\Madness Project Nexus\Mods`.
3. Steam: right-click MADNESS: Project Nexus, Properties, Betas, pick `releasebeta`, and let it update.
4. Launch, click the gear icon at the bottom left of the main menu, select Director (all game modes), tick ENABLED, Apply, restart the game.

Copying the `SwapUnaware` folder (`info.txt` and `Managed/SwapUnaware.dll`) into that Mods folder by hand does the same.

## Build

Needs the .NET SDK and the game installed. `pwsh ./pack.ps1 -Version 0.6.0` builds `src/` against the game's own `Managed` DLLs (net48, `Private=false`, nothing copied from the game) and writes `dist/SwapUnaware-<version>.zip`. It finds the game with the hub skill's `mpn.py where`; pass `-GameManaged <...\Madness Project Nexus_Data\Managed>` or set `MPN_MANAGED` to point elsewhere. Then deploy with `python -I ../../skills/madnesswright/scripts/mpn.py deploy dist/stage/SwapUnaware --force` and read `mpn.py log` after playing.

To update the Workshop item after a new build (absolute paths; relative ones end in `result FileNotFound`): `../WorkshopUpload/out/wsup.exe <abs>/dist/stage/SwapUnaware <abs>/preview.jpg "Director (was Playground Director)" workshop-description.txt "Scripts" 3822329165` (see [WorkshopUpload](../WorkshopUpload/README.md)).

`build/`, `dist/`, `bin/` and `obj/` stay out of git. Never commit game DLLs or decompiled code.

## How it works

- `DirectorMenu` (INSERT) holds a list of `MenuEntry` (label, key, modes, pick a character first, or an action). Each `Feature` adds its own lines in `Register()`; a keyed line with no action presses that key for the feature for one frame (`Feature.Pressed`), so a new feature needs one `Menu(...)` call and no new button code. Scenes open straight from the menu because they need the pause.
- `ModEntry.Initialize()` adds one component per feature to a host object (`Feature` base class: skips while paused, logs exceptions, switches only itself off after five in a row) plus a one-line on-screen message (`Hud`). Shared helpers (pick under the mouse, playable check, claim release) live in `Game`.
- F2 locks each controller the way cutscenes do (`Controller_Base.LockController`), clears waypoints, pins the rigidbody (`SetFrozen`) and sets the animator speed to 0; all four are undone on F2, on death and when the player takes the body.
- F1 calls `Combatant_Base.RemoveFromGame()` after releasing claims. F10 uses `Controller_Base.SetAlertStatus` and `AI_Manager.SetKillTarget`. F9 copies the spawner's FixedInPlace (`wanderingDisabled`, `SetFrozen(true)`). F4 plays `Combatant_Base.PlayAsEmote(clip, true, false)` with clips from `myAnimSet.IdleEmotes` and `LibraryStorageList.Library.AnimClips`. F3 walks with `AI_Manager.AssignWalkToPosition` and checks arrival by distance. F11 writes each flat record with `JsonUtility` and joins the nesting itself (`JsonUtility` drops the mod's own nested classes), holds its own pause source, closes the game's pause menu, and loads through `InteractiveMode.ClearAll_DoIt()`, `Char_Data.CreateCharacter`, `Combatant_Base.CreateCombatant`, `Squad_Main.AddMember` and `Char_DataAssigner.GiveItemToCharacter`. Weapons (`SceneItems.cs`): loose `Item_Held` objects with no living owner are saved and on load `DeSpawn`ed and laid back with `Item_Held.CreateHeld`; loadouts are emptied and re-given, then `SetAmmo`, `SetMags` and `SetMeleeAmmo` restore the ammo; anything `amRegistered` (a career's weapon) is skipped.
- `AttackBlockPatch` skips `Controller_Player.RunControls_Attacks()` while the patrol tool owns the mouse; the game has no "mouse over UI" check of its own.
- F5 picks by screen distance from the mouse over every living NPC, because the game's own `Combat_Manager.ReturnClosestTarget` only accepts targets in your current room. Squadmates go through the game's own `Controller_Player.SwapControllersWithSquadmate(Combatant_Base)` (skipping the core-roster check, which only lives in `CanBePlayerControlled`); anyone else gets `Combatant_Base.AssignController`.
- One Harmony prefix (`LossSwapPatch`) on `Controller_Player.SwapControllersWithSquadmate(bool)`, which the game calls with `overrideChecks: true` when the controlled body dies or leaves the squad: it returns you to your own body, and skips the game's code when the body had no squad.
- F6 releases each NPC's target claim through the game's `AI_TargetClaimer.UnclaimMyTarget` and sets `AI_Wakeup.AlertStatus.Unaware`.

Members checked against beta v2.20.a (build 25615196, 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-21); re-check them in a fresh decompile after a game update.
