Director 0.14.0

Build a wave: new "Max enemies alive at once" setting under the list (use - and +; it starts at no limit). With a limit, only that many of the wave's enemies are alive at a time: when one dies, the next one in the list comes in, until the whole list has spawned. The window also shows how many enemies the wave has in all.

Each enemy now has a "Dual wielding: off/on" switch. Only when it is on does the Off hand weapon picker show and the enemy get a second weapon. If the game won't let that character hold the pair, the row says so and the enemy carries it as a backup. The Throwable picker is unchanged.

Waves saved with 0.13.0 still load: no limit, and enemies that had a 2nd weapon load with dual wielding on.
