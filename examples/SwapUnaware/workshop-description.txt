[b]Take control of any character and make everyone else stop noticing you.[/b]

[list]
[*][b]F5[/b]: take control of the character nearest the mouse pointer: a squadmate or an enemy, in any room (through locked arena doors too). Your old body becomes an NPC. Works while you are dead.
[*][b]F8[/b]: back to your own body. Happens by itself when the body you took dies, if your own body is still alive.
[*][b]F6[/b]: every other character goes back to unaware and forgets who it was fighting.
[*][b]F7[/b]: keep everyone unaware until you press F7 again.
[/list]

[b]Needs the releasebeta branch[/b] (Steam: Properties, Betas, releasebeta): the normal release build does not load mods. Enable it from the gear icon on the main menu and restart.

Made for the Playground; not tested in the story career, so back up your saves before using it there.

This mod runs code (a Harmony DLL). Source, build steps and the bugs each playtest found: https://github.com/m4bwav/madnesswright/tree/main/examples/SwapUnaware
