[b]Direct any fight: take over anyone, freeze the room, set patrols and guards, pose enemies, clear the bodies, and save scenes to play again, in the story, the arenas and the Playground.[/b] Not just for the Playground: every key works in the story campaign too. (Formerly Playground Director and Swap And Unaware: the keys work as before.)

[list]
[*][b]INSERT[/b]: the Director menu. Pauses the game and lists every action with its key, plus [b]Clear corpses[/b] (keeps your squad, bosses and bodies the level still needs) and [b]Clear blood, bullet holes, casings and debris[/b]. Actions aimed at one character ask you to click it (right click cancels). INSERT or ESC closes it.
[*][b]F1[/b]: remove every living character except you and the playable characters (Sanford, Deimos, Hank and the rest).
[*][b]F2[/b]: freeze everyone except the character you are playing. F2 again: unfreeze them. Frozen characters stay frozen when F10 changes their alert or you take control and switch away; they still die when shot.
[*][b]F3[/b]: patrol squares, like Ancient Warfare 3's patrol mode. A black square follows the mouse; left click drops it (no shot); [b]U[/b] starts another; click a square to select it, then [b]P[/b] turns it red (the unit stops there) and [b]Delete[/b] removes it. F3 hides and shows them. The squares belong to the character you are playing when you place them and only show while you play that character; whenever someone else controls it and it has not spotted an enemy, it walks them.
[*][b]F4[/b]: point at a character: a list of idle animations (typing, leaning, smoking and the rest). Click one and it plays it. F4 or ESC closes the list.
[*][b]F5[/b]: take control of the character nearest the mouse pointer: a squadmate or an enemy, in any room. Your old body becomes an NPC. Works while you are dead.
[*][b]F6[/b]: every other character goes back to unaware and forgets who it was fighting.
[*][b]F7[/b]: keep everyone unaware until you press F7 again.
[*][b]F8[/b]: back to your own body. Happens by itself when the body you took dies.
[*][b]F9[/b]: point at a character: it stands guard, never moves, and attacks anyone not on its team once it sees them. F9 again: back to normal.
[*][b]F10[/b]: point at a character: first press it searches, second it attacks you, third it loses track of you, then round again.
[*][b]F11[/b] while paused: type a scene name, [b]ENTER[/b] saves it. [b]CAPS LOCK[/b] while paused lists your saved scenes; click one to load it. A scene keeps every character, where they stand, their team, weapons and ammo, damage, the animation they were playing (typing, smoking, an F4 pose), and frozen, guard and patrol settings, plus the weapons lying on the floor; loading puts the weapons back too, picks up thrown ones and resets the ammo (in a story or arena career your own weapons stay yours and only their ammo resets). In the Playground loading rebuilds the room. In story levels and arenas it moves you back to where you saved, puts the characters back and respawns the dead (back in their saved squad, so teammates stay teammates), starts their animations again, and clears the bodies, blood, bullet holes and casings made since the save; if the game has locked that room, restart the stage and load again. Scenes saved before 0.7.0: save them again to keep weapons.
[/list]

[b]Two players[/b] (new in 0.8.0): works with the game's local co-op. A second player joins on a gamepad with Start (answer No to "Control Player One?"). The keyboard player drives the Director; F2 does not freeze a player's body, and no player is guarded, cleared or made a target. If you take over an already frozen body, it stays frozen until F2. A scene load puts each player back on their saved spot with their weapons.

[b]Arena co-op[/b] (new in 0.9.0): in an arena, a second player who presses Start on a pad gets a panel listing the origins your career has unlocked (D-pad browse, A pick, B cancel) and plays a guest of that origin at your level, beside you. The guest is not saved in your career, its death never ends the wave, and it is back once the wave is over.

[b]Keep my character and weapons[/b] (new in 0.10.0): a box at the top of the CAPS LOCK scene list. Ticked, loading a scene leaves the character you play and the weapons you hold alone, even after loading a scene from another Playground map. Off by default and remembered between games.

[b]Needs the releasebeta branch[/b] (Steam: Properties, Betas, releasebeta): the normal release build does not load mods. Enable it from the gear icon on the main menu and restart.

Works in the story mode, the arenas, custom stages and the Playground. In an arena, F1 counts as kills and moves the waves on. Arenas cannot be paused between waves, so save scenes mid-wave or before the first. Back up your saves before using it in a story career.
